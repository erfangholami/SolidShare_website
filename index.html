How it works

Three steps, and your pod stays yours

Solid is an open web standard for personal data stores, called pods. You pick the provider, or you run your own server.

  1. 1

    Pick a pod

    Use Inrupt, Solid Community, Data Pod, or any custom OIDC issuer URL. You can log into several pods at once and switch between them.

  2. 2

    Sign in with Solid-OIDC

    The app authenticates directly with the provider you picked. From then on it reads and writes resources on your pod, from your phone.

  3. 3

    Share on your terms

    Grant a specific WebID, or anyone with the link, View, Add or Edit access. Widen, narrow or revoke the share at any time.

Features

One app for the things you keep

Files

Browse containers and resources in list or grid, sorted by name, type or date.

  • Upload from device storage, or capture a photo or video and upload it straight away
  • Download a resource and open it with any compatible app
  • Duplicate a file or folder as a private copy, reset to owner-only access
  • Pin a file to keep its content available offline

Sharing

Every share produces a QR code and a link that opens straight back into the app.

  • Grant access to a specific Solid user by WebID, or to anyone with the link
  • View, Add and Edit access levels, instead of raw Read, Append and Write
  • Two pod-backed lists: what you have shared, and what has been shared with you
  • Ask an owner for access when a resource denies you, and answer requests yourself
  • Works across Solid servers, with Web Access Control and an ACP fallback

Wallet

Event tickets, boarding passes, cinema tickets, loyalty cards and coupons, stored as ordinary Solid resources you own.

  • Apple Wallet .pkpass import, read in full, with the original file kept on your pod
  • Barcodes re-rendered in the symbology the issuer used, so gate scanners read the identical code
  • A screen-brightness boost when you open a pass
  • An open ticket-QR format, so any issuer can offer one-tap add-to-pod

Contacts

Your address book on your pod, in the standard vCard vocabulary, round-tripped in full.

  • Two-way sync with the phone's Contacts app, one Android account per pod
  • Import from the phone's own accounts, or from a .vcf file
  • Export everything back out at any time
  • Near-duplicates are suggested for review, and merged only when you say so

Offline first

Everything you have seen is on the device, in an encrypted database, and opens with no connection. Every write queues and drains by itself when the connection comes back.

Several pods at once

Log into more than one pod and switch between them. An expired session is a state, not a logout — reconnect in one tap and keep your data in place.

Notifications

The recipient is told when their access changes. Access requests arrive as notifications you can accept or decline.

Open standards

Solid Protocol, Solid-OIDC, Web Access Control with an ACP fallback, and vCard. Your data stays readable by any other Solid client.
